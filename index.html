V-Taper PPL

Cardio plan

On a cut, cardio adds to your calorie burn. Lifting is still what keeps the muscle, so keep cardio easy enough that it never hurts your sessions.

Weekly layout

Daily

8–10k steps. This is your main fat-loss tool after diet.

Tue

25 min incline walk or bike after Pull A. Easy pace: you could talk in full sentences.

Fri

25 min incline walk or bike after Pull B, same easy pace.

Sat

Optional sprint work after Legs B. Follow the progression below.

Sun

Rest. A relaxed walk is fine.

Sprint progression

Warm up first every time: 5 min jog, leg swings, A-skips. Never sprint flat out while the hernia is unrepaired.

Wk 1–4

6–8 strides of 60–80 m at about 70% effort. Walk back to recover.

Wk 5–8

4–6 hill sprints, 10–15 seconds at about 85%. Hills cut groin stress.

Wk 9+

4–6 flat sprints of 40–60 m at about 90%, with 2–3 min rest between.

If fat loss stalls

Add 2,000 daily steps or a third 25-minute session before cutting food further. Change one thing at a time and give it two weeks.

How to progress

  1. Start with a weight you can lift for the bottom of the rep range.
  2. Add reps each week until every set reaches the top of the range.
  3. Then add the smallest jump (2.5 kg, or the next dumbbell) and repeat. The app tells you when.
  4. Compounds stop 1–2 reps short of failure. Isolation work goes to 0–1 reps short.
  5. Deload every 6–8 weeks, or if strength drops two sessions in a row: same exercises, half the sets.

On a cut, holding your numbers steady is a win.

How XP and ranks work

  • 10 XP per set, 50 XP per personal record, 100 XP for finishing a workout, plus bonus XP for badges.
  • A PR means your best estimated one-rep max on that lift went up. It's worked out from weight and reps, so more reps at the same weight counts.
  • Lift ranks compare your best to your first logged session: Silver at +5%, Gold +10%, Platinum +20%, Diamond +35%, Champion +50%.
  • The body map averages the ranks of every lift for each muscle. It shows which muscles are progressing fastest and which are lagging, not your strength against other people.
  • Swaps and extra exercises keep their own history, so going back to the regular plan never loses your numbers.

Cut nutrition

  • Lose about 0.5–1% of bodyweight a week, closer to 0.5% as you get leaner.
  • Protein around 2–2.2 g per kg of bodyweight, spread over 3–4 meals.
  • Keep most carbs around your training.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours. Short sleep makes a cut cost more muscle.

Hernia and back rules

  • No max-effort singles or grinding reps, especially squats, RDLs and leg press. PRs here come from reps, not maxing out.
  • Breathe out through the hard part of each rep instead of holding your breath.
  • Stop the exercise if you feel a bulge, dragging or sharp pain in your groin.
  • If rows or RDLs flare your lower back, swap to chest-supported rows and lying leg curls.
  • Get the night-time back pain checked by a GP or physio if it continues.

Where this comes from

The structure follows peer-reviewed hypertrophy research. Exercise choice follows what you enjoy, because research rarely picks one best exercise and consistency matters more.

Jeff Nippard's programmes are built on much of this same research. Arnold's split is classic bodybuilding: high volume, each muscle twice a week, proven in practice more than in studies.

Key findings used

Train each muscle twice a week

A 2016 meta-analysis found twice-weekly training grew more muscle than once a week.

Schoenfeld et al., 2016 · Sports Medicine
More weekly sets, more growth, up to a point

Growth rose with weekly volume, with 10+ sets per muscle doing best. A later review suggested 12–20 weekly sets for trained men.

Schoenfeld et al., 2017 · J Sports Sciences Baz-Valle et al., 2022 · J Human Kinetics
Overhead triceps work beats pushdowns alone

Over 12 weeks, the triceps long head grew about 28.5% with overhead extensions versus 19.6% with pushdowns.

Maeo et al., 2023 · Eur J Sport Science
Protein benefits level off around 1.6 g/kg

A 49-study meta-analysis found little extra muscle gain above about 1.6 g/kg a day. Cutting raises needs, so this plan aims a little higher.

Morton et al., 2018 · Br J Sports Med
Lose weight slowly to keep muscle

A review of natural bodybuilding prep recommends losing about 0.5–1% of bodyweight a week.

Helms et al., 2014 · JISSN
